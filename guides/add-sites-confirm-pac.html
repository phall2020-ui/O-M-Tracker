How to add a site and confirm PAC

A short picture guide for ADE people with manager access, using the live tracker you log into (not a spreadsheet, and not a test copy). Two jobs: get the site onto the pipeline, then tick it as accepted when PAC is signed off.

Job 1Get the site onto the pipeline Job 2Confirm PAC (Accepted by O&M)

Pipeline

Sites we know are coming, but O&M has not accepted them yet. They must not be billed.

PAC

Provisional / Practical Acceptance. ADE has accepted the finished site. From this date Clearsol looks after it.

Sites

The operational list. A site only appears here after someone ticks Accepted by O&M. That is when fees start.

Open the live tracker. Sign in at the O&M Portfolio Tracker (AMPYR Distributed Energy). Use your work login. If you cannot see Pipeline on the left, or you cannot see the Accepted by O&M boxes, you do not have manager access yet — ask Peter Hall.

Sign in

You will see a dark screen titled O&M Portfolio Tracker.

  1. Type your email and password.
  2. Click the orange Sign in button.
  3. You land on the Dashboard. That is normal. The two pages you need are Pipeline and Sites on the left.

1 Enter your email and password. 2 Click Sign in.

1 Sites is the live, billed portfolio. 2 Pipeline is the waiting list. Use Pipeline for both jobs below.

1Get the site onto the pipeline

Do this as soon as you know a site is coming (in build, or getting close to handover). Leave Accepted by O&M unticked until PAC is actually accepted.

  1. Click Pipeline on the left.
  2. Type the site name in Search pipeline...
  3. If it is already listed, stop. Do not add it twice. You are done with Job 1.
  4. If it is not listed: this live tracker has no Add Site button. Ask Peter Hall (or whoever maintains the tracker data) to load the site onto Pipeline, not onto Sites.
Why there is no Add button. On the live tracker, new sites are loaded into the database and then appear on Pipeline. Manager access lets you accept them and edit what is already there. It does not currently give a form to create a brand-new row.

1 Open Pipeline. 2 Search for the name first. 3 Check the status. Awaiting Contract, Awaiting PAC and even Contracted can all still sit here until the O&M box is ticked.

You are done with Job 1 when: the site is on Pipeline, Accepted by O&M is empty, and the monthly fee is still £0. It will not count as billed until Job 2.

2Confirm PAC (tick Accepted by O&M)

Only do this when ADE has accepted PAC. If there are still open items (missing drawings, a PR test, and so on) keep the box unticked and leave the site on the pipeline.

Before you tick: the PAC letter should be filed, and you should be happy for Clearsol to start looking after the site from that date. Ticking the box auto-saves. The site then leaves Pipeline and appears under Sites, and fees start.
  1. Go to Pipeline.
  2. Find the site. You can click View first if you want to check the details. You should see Awaiting O&M acceptance and costs marked Excluded until accepted by O&M.
  3. Go back to the Pipeline list.
  4. Tick the box in the Accepted by O&M column for that row.
  5. Wait a moment. It saves itself. You do not click a separate Save button.

1 View shows the site is still waiting. 2 Fees stay at £0 and are marked excluded until you accept it. There is no Accept button on this page — go back to the Pipeline list.

1 Tick Accepted by O&M on the Pipeline list. That is the PAC confirmation. The site then moves to Sites and starts billing.

1 After you tick the box, the site belongs on Sites. 2 Status should read Contracted and a monthly fee should show.

You have confirmed PAC when: the site has left Pipeline, it appears under Sites as Contracted, a monthly fee is showing, and the Dashboard contracted count has gone up.
