How to add a site and confirm PAC

A short picture guide for ADE people with manager access. Two jobs: put a new site on the pipeline, then tick it as contracted when PAC is signed off.

Job 1Add a site to the pipeline Job 2Confirm PAC (contracted)

The pipeline

Sites we know are coming, but ADE has not yet accepted them. They should not be billed yet.

PAC

Provisional Acceptance. ADE has accepted the finished site. From this date Clearsol looks after it.

Contracted

Same thing as PAC in this tracker. Monthly fees only start once the site is contracted.

Need manager access. If you cannot see a red Add Site button, ask Peter Hall or your manager to give you edit rights before you start.

1Add a site to the pipeline

Do this as soon as you know a site is coming (in build, or getting close to handover). Leave it as not contracted until PAC is actually accepted.

  1. Open the tracker and click Sites on the left.
  2. Search for the name first. If it is already there, skip to Job 2. Do not add it twice.
  3. Click the red Add Site button.

1 Click Sites in the menu. 2 Or use Add New Site on the home page.

3 Click Add Site for a new site. 4 To change an existing site later, pick it from this list at the bottom.

  1. Fill in the site name and size.
  2. Leave Contract Status (PAC achieved?) as No.
  3. Leave the onboard date blank.
  4. Add the company (SPV) if you know it. Costs can wait.
  5. Click Create Site.

5 Keep this on No until PAC is signed off. 6 Click Create Site.

You are done with Job 1 when: the site appears on the Sites list, Contract says No, and there is no monthly fee yet.

2Confirm PAC (the site is contracted)

Only do this when ADE has accepted PAC. If there are still open items (missing drawings, a PR test, and so on) keep the site on the pipeline.

Before you click: the PAC letter should be filed, and you should be happy for Clearsol to start looking after the site from that date.
  1. Go to Sites and find the site (you can set Status to Pipeline (not PAC) to see only waiting sites).
  2. Choose the site at the bottom of the page and click Edit Site.
  3. Change Contract Status (PAC achieved?) to Yes.
  4. Put the PAC date in Onboard Date. This is required.
  5. Click Save Changes.

1 Switch to Yes. 2 Enter the PAC date. 3 Save. Fees start from this date.

You have confirmed PAC when: Contract says Yes, the onboard date is the PAC date, a monthly fee is showing, and the home page contracted count has gone up.
